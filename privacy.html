PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Who we are

This website, dallagames.com, is run by Dalla Games Inc., the developer of AeonFall. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to dalla.gamedev@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

Visit statisticsWe use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used: pages viewed, the site that sent you here, approximate country and city, device and browser type, and actions on this page (for example pressing a Steam button, playing the trailer, changing the language, opening an FAQ answer, how far you scroll). Google Analytics 4 does not log or store IP addresses. We use this only to improve the site and our announcements.
Your languageThe language you choose is saved in your own browser (local storage) so the site remembers it. It is not sent to us.
EmailIf you email us, we use your address and message only to reply to you.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use advertising cookies.

Cookies

_ga, _ga_<ID>Google Analytics. Distinguishes one visitor from another. Expires after up to 2 years.
aeon-langLocal storage. Remembers the language you chose. Necessary.

If you do not want to be counted, you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google’s Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

Other services on the page

  • YouTube — the trailer is loaded from youtube-nocookie.com only when you press play. From then on YouTube’s own privacy policy applies.
  • Steam — Steam buttons take you to Valve’s store, where Valve’s privacy policy applies.
  • Hosting — the site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may keep technical logs (such as IP addresses) for security.

Where the data goes, and how long we keep it

Visit statistics are processed by Google LLC, including on servers in the United States. Statistics data is kept for no longer than 14 months and is then deleted automatically. Emails are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry.

Your rights

You can stop being counted at any time as described under “Cookies” above. You can also ask us to tell you what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it, by emailing dalla.gamedev@gmail.com. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.

수집하는 정보와 목적

방문 통계Google Analytics 4 로 방문 통계를 수집합니다. 본 페이지, 유입 경로(어느 사이트에서 왔는지), 대략적인 국가·도시, 기기·브라우저 종류, 페이지 안의 행동(스팀 버튼 클릭, 트레일러 재생, 언어 변경, FAQ 열람, 스크롤 깊이 등)이 해당됩니다. Google Analytics 4 는 IP 주소를 기록·저장하지 않습니다. 사이트와 소식 안내를 개선하는 데만 사용합니다.
언어 선택고른 언어는 방문자의 브라우저(로컬 저장소)에만 저장되며, 저희에게 전송되지 않습니다.
이메일 문의메일을 보내 주시면 답장을 위해서만 주소와 내용을 사용합니다.

개인정보를 판매하지 않으며, 광고용 쿠키를 사용하지 않습니다.

쿠키

페이지 안의 외부 서비스
